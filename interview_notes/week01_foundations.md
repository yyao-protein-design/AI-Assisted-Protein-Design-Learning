What is AlphaFold?



\# AlphaFold is an AI tool for predicting protein structure from its sequence. It generates a few structures, each with scores that approximate likelihood of it being accurate.



What is ProteinMPNN?



\# ProteinMPNN generates sequence from a designed protein morphology. It outputs a sequence that may fold into the input structure.



What is RFdiffusion?



\# RFdiffusion generates protein structure/topology that bind/accommodate small molecules/cofactors/other proteins.



What is ESM?



\# ESM predicts protein folding structure from its sequence.



Why can't AlphaFold design proteins?



\# Alphafold is only trained with the protein sequence and folding results, and it predicts the latter from the former. It can predict the structure of de novo proteins, but it does not generate new sequences.



Why can't ProteinMPNN replace RFdiffusion?



\# ProteinMPNN needs a structure as input, RFdiffusion generates this structure from design concepts.
